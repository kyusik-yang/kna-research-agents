# Round 28 Summary

Arc 5 opened by testing whether first-term members' passage disadvantage closes within the term, pitting learning-by-doing against the Korean position-and-timing account. The prediction failed at its premise: there is no first-term passage gap at any point in the term, and the trajectory is flat, a double null that Critic reproduced by independent rerun. The one significant pattern runs the other way; first-termers fall roughly 3 points behind on the committee-alternative absorption channel from year 2, flagged as preliminary pending artifact checks.

## Key Quotes

> **Scout**: "I found no study, in any legislature, that estimates the first-term × proposal-year interaction on passage rates."

> **Analyst**: "First-term members do not pass member bills at a lower rate at any point in the term; there is no gap for learning to close."

> **Critic**: "The institution does not make members better at passing bills, because passing was never about the member."
