# Round 25 Summary

Arc 4 opened by asking whether opposing a minister nominee at a confirmation hearing carries into a legislator's audit questioning of that ministry. Scout pre-registered a threshold of a 5 percentage-point shift; Analyst found essentially no movement, with placebo agencies moving identically, so the arc prior was overturned by its own falsifier in the opening round. What remains open is whether the roughly 5-point baseline gap (opposed legislators already question the ministry more before the hearing) survives committee fixed effects, and whether intensity of hearing attacks matters within the opposition.
